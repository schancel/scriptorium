Your stage

The stage decides which letters are lit. Setting it yourself is the honest way to skip ahead if you already type — nothing else moves it except earning it.

Gilding is for people who already type. With it on, every character is required and nothing skips ahead on its own; the letters your stage has not taught turn gold as you type them, and filling a whole part earns gold leaf. It does not move your stage — the mastery gate measures only the keys your stage teaches, in either mode.

The ink cloud

Stop typing altogether and a cloud of ink drifts in and smudges finished work. Taking a long time over a single key never brings it — only stopping does. Turn it off if it nags rather than nudges; nothing else changes, and the game is fully playable without it.
